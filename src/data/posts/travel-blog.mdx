# 48 Hours in Shinjuku

Tokyo is a city that never truly sleeps. From the neon lights of the Golden Gai to the quiet morning mist in Meiji Jingu, every corner tells a story.

### Highlights of the Trip

1. **Tsukiji Outer Market:** The freshest sushi I've ever tasted.
2. **Shibuya Crossing:** A chaotic symphony of humans.
3. **Mount Fuji:** A lucky clear view from the city.

### What I Learned

Tokyo taught me that urban life and natural beauty can coexist. The city's dedication to tradition while embracing modernity is truly inspiring. I can't wait to return and explore more hidden gems.
