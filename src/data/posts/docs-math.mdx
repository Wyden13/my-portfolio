# Calculating Force

In classical mechanics, the relationship between mass and acceleration is defined by Newton's Second Law:

$$F = m \cdot a$$

Where:

- $F$ is the force applied.
- $m$ is the mass of the object.
- $a$ is the acceleration.

### Try it Yourself

This equation is fundamental to understanding motion and forces in the physical world. By manipulating the mass or acceleration values, you can observe how the force changes proportionally.
